01 Selected work

Products I'm building now

Mobile app · iOS

Nutoro

Food logging that stays out of your way.

A clean, fast calorie and macro tracker. Build a meal on a “plate”, see its totals, then log it in one tap onto a daily timeline. Search, barcode and nutrition-label scanning, recipes, and optional AI that logs a meal from a photo or a quick description.

  • React Native
  • TypeScript
  • Expo
  • Self-hosted Supabase
Visit nutoroapp.com ↗
Mobile app · App Store

HealthCore

Your Apple Health data, finally readable.

A dashboard for Apple Health that turns raw data into trends and insights: net calories, sleep stages, protein, steps and weight, by day, week, month or year. Streaks and medals reward the habits you build, and all of your data stays on your device.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • HealthKit
View on the App Store
Web platform · AI

SetupSignal

Watch an AI trader work a real market, in the open.

An AI paper-trading platform. A self-improving AI day trader works BTC and MNQ on a simulated account and publishes every trade idea, its reasoning, a journal and performance analytics, with Discord alerts when it plans a trade. Built as a study tool, not financial advice.

  • Next.js
  • TypeScript
  • PostgreSQL + pgvector
  • Docker
Visit setupsignal.com ↗

03 About

Four years in, still hooked.

I've been a software engineer for over four years, and I've been hooked on tech since I was a kid. Finding programming felt like finding my calling, and that spark carried me through a degree in computer science and into building software full time.

I love puzzles and the unknown. Most of what I build starts with a problem in my own life: fitness and gaming are my two biggest passions, which is why my side projects tend to be health apps and game-jam entries. Lately I've been running my own products on infrastructure I set up and maintain myself.
