Nutoro

Food logging that stays out of your way

Most calorie trackers make you log food one item at a time. In Nutoro you put a whole meal on a plate, check the totals and log it in one tap. When typing is too much effort, scan the barcode or snap a photo and let AI fill it in.

HealthCore

Apple Health, but readable

Your iPhone collects a lot of health data and shows you very little of it. HealthCore turns it into trends you can read at a glance, like sleep stages and net calories, and hands out streaks for the habits you're working on. Your data never leaves the phone.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • HealthKit

Little letters, delivered by a pig

You write a stack of love notes, and a pixel pig delivers one a day to someone you care about. Each letter they read grows a sunflower in their garden. In between, they can feed the pig and teach it tricks. The original Firebase version just got a full rebuild.

SetupSignal

An AI trader that shows its work

An AI day trades BTC and MNQ on a simulated account and gets better from its own results. Everything happens in the open: each trade idea, the reasoning behind it and a running journal. It's a study tool for people learning to trade, not financial advice.

03 About

A bit about me

Most of my side projects start with something in my own life. I lift and I game, so I end up building health apps and entering game jams. I like owning the whole stack, right down to the server my apps run on.
